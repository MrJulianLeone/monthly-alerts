# Meta / Instagram Ads: MonthlyAlerts.com

Budget: $500 to $750 per month. Objective: Sales (website conversions), optimizing
for `project_activated` once volume allows, `project_created` before that.
Placements: Instagram Reels, Instagram Stories, Facebook Reels, Facebook Feed,
Instagram Feed. Everything else off.

Three ads. Each is 10 to 20 seconds, vertical 9:16, silent-readable, shot by one
person with a phone and a screen recorder.

### Lookalike plan

Do not build lookalikes yet. Meta needs at least 100 source records and they need
to be the right records.

| Milestone | Build |
|---|---|
| 100 `signup` events | 1% LAL US from Website Custom Audience `signup` (180-day window). Test against Ad Set 2's interest targeting, same creative. |
| 50 `project_activated` events | 1% LAL US from a Customer List upload of paying owners (email, exported from the app database). This is the highest-value source. Also build 2% and 3% but run them only after 1% beats interests. |
| 250 site visitors on `/for-contractors` | 1% LAL from that page's Custom Audience, run in Ad Set 1 only. |
| Any time after week 4 | Retargeting ad set: visited any page in 30 days AND did not fire `project_created`. Cap at $3/day. Use Ad 1. |

Suppression: always exclude the Customer List of paying owners from all
prospecting ad sets. The product is per-project, so a repeat purchase is
possible, but a separate email works better than paid impressions.

## Creative testing plan

Four-week cycles. One variable per cycle. Never test creative and audience in the
same week.

### Week 1-2: baseline

All three ads live, one per ad set, as specified above. Do nothing else. Look at
3-second video plays and hook rate (3-sec plays / impressions). Below 25% hook
rate means the first two seconds failed, not the offer.

### Week 3-4: hook test

For the best-performing ad set, produce two alternate first-2-second openers,
same body. Ad 1 openers to test:
- A: current (typing in Spanish)
- B: text-only cold open on a black card, `Your crew reads Spanish. Your client reads English.`
- C: split screen, both languages visible from frame one

Kill any variant under 20% hook rate after 1,000 impressions.

### Week 5-6: end-card test

Same winning hook, two end cards:
- A: `$100 per project. Not a subscription.`
- B: `Your first project is on us.` (only if the free-first-project offer from
  `contractor-outreach.md` is extended to paid traffic; if not, skip this test)

### Week 7-8: format test

Winning ad recut as:
- A: 12s (current)
- B: 20s with a second proof beat (the monthly status email shown in full)
- C: 4:5 static carousel, 3 cards: chaos, one list, price

### Rules

| Metric | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3s plays / impressions) | under 20% after 1,000 impressions | Kill the creative |
| Cost per landing page view | over $1.50 | Rewrite the primary text, not the video |
| Frequency | over 2.5 in 7 days | Refresh creative or widen geo |
| Cost per `project_created` | over $35 for 14 days | Pause the ad set |
| Any ad set | zero `project_created` at $150 cumulative | Pause |

Refresh creative every 4 weeks regardless of performance. Reels audiences burn
fast at small budgets because the addressable pool is narrow.

## What a solo founder can actually record

Everything above is shootable in one afternoon with a phone, a laptop and free
software. No crew, no actors, no studio.

### Setup

1. **Seed a demo project.** Create a real project in MonthlyAlerts called
   something plausible ("Maple St Kitchen Remodel"). Use the kitchen remodel
   template. Add 12 to 15 items, 3 photos, 2 comments, and set 2 items overdue.
   Create three test accounts, one per language (EN, IT, ES), and invite them.
   The ad only works if the screen looks like a real job. An empty demo reads as
   vaporware.
2. **Two devices, two languages.** Phone A signed in as the Spanish-language
   member, phone B as the English-language member. That gives you the flip in
   Ad 1 without any fake mockups.

### Screen recording

| Need | Tool | How |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone screen | Built-in Screen Recording (Settings, Control Center, add Screen Recording) | Record at native resolution, portrait. Turn on Do Not Disturb first so no notification banner ruins the take. |
| iPhone screen, clean and tethered | QuickTime Player on Mac, File, New Movie Recording, select the iPhone as camera | Gives you a full-resolution capture with no status-bar clutter, easier to edit |
| Mac screen (for the desktop list view) | Cmd+Shift+5 | Record a 1080x1920 region so you do not have to crop later |
| Talking head (Ad 3 only) | Phone rear camera, propped on anything, filming yourself in a mirror is not needed; use the front camera and accept it | Shoot near a window, face the light, phone at eye level, arm's length |

### Editing

| Tool | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CapCut (desktop or mobile) | Free | 9:16 timeline preset, text presets, easy speed ramps for the typing shot, one-click burn-in captions you then correct by hand |
| iMovie | Free | Fine if you only need cuts and text cards |
| Descript | Paid | Only worth it if you add voiceover later |

CapCut is the right default. Set the project to 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps.

### Concrete production order

1. Record the Spanish comment being typed on phone A. One take, 20 seconds. Do
   it three times, keep the cleanest.
2. Record the English view of the same thread on phone B, scrolling slowly.
3. Record a 20-second slow scroll of the full checklist in English.
4. Screenshot your own WhatsApp, Messages, Gmail and Notes with plausible remodel
   content. Five screenshots is enough for the Ad 2 pile-up.
5. Screenshot the monthly status email in your inbox.
6. Film the Ad 3 opener line. Say it eight times. Keep take 6 or 7; the first
   five will be stiff.
7. Assemble in CapCut. Speed the typing shot to 1.5x. Use hard cuts only. No
   crossfades anywhere in any of the three ads.
8. Add text: Inter or Helvetica, white on a black bar or with a hard shadow, 56pt
   minimum at 1080 width. No script fonts, no outlines, no animation beyond a
   1-frame pop.
9. Export 1080x1920 MP4, then export a 4:5 version by re-framing, not cropping
   the export.
10. Watch each ad muted on a phone before uploading. If the point is not clear
    muted, the ad is not done.

### Non-negotiables

- Every Spanish and Italian line must be correct. Have a native speaker read the
  burn-ins once. A translation error in a translation ad is fatal.
- No stock footage of hard hats. Show the actual product screen.
- No claims beyond the site: one checklist, automatic translation of items,
  comments and photo captions, photos on items, per-phase budgets, project PDFs,
  monthly status email, roles (owner, editor, commenter), templates, $100 one
  time per project, invitees free, two years of storage. Nothing else.
