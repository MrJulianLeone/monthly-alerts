# Organic Social Calendar: 8 Weeks

Channels: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts. Nothing else. No paid LinkedIn, no
X, no TikTok until the other three are running without effort.

Cadence: 3 posts per week. Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Every post goes to all three
channels; Reels cross-post to Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts unchanged.

Goal: demonstrate the problem. Not brand-building, not follower count. The metric
that matters is profile-link clicks that turn into `project_created`. A post with
400 views and 6 link clicks beats a post with 40,000 views and 2.

## Pillars

| Pillar | What it is | Slot | Format default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Translation examples** | "The contractor said this. The homeowner sees this." Real EN/ES and EN/IT pairs on screen. | Monday | Reel / Short, 8-15s |
| **B. Construction tips** | Genuinely useful lists for homeowners and small contractors. No product mention in the body. | Wednesday | Carousel (6-10 cards) |
| **C. Project organization** | The cost of running a job across 14 threads, and what one list changes. | Friday | Reel or static, alternating |

## Production rules

- Every Reel is silent-readable. Burn in all text.
- Every Spanish and Italian line gets checked by a native speaker before posting.
  One wrong translation in a translation post ends the account's credibility.
- Pillar B posts do not sell. The bio link does the selling.
- Bio link: `https://www.monthlyalerts.com/` (swap to `/for-contractors` during
  weeks when Monday and Friday both target contractors).
- Batch production: record all four Monday Reels for a month in one sitting.
- Reuse: any Pillar A Reel that clears 3x median views becomes a Meta ad creative
  candidate. Cross-reference `meta-ads.md`.

## Weekly measurement

Track five numbers per post in a single sheet. Nothing else.

| Column | Source |
|---|---|
| Post ID (W3-Fri) | manual |
| Pillar (A/B/C) | manual |
| Format | manual |
| Views / plays | IG Insights, YT Studio |
| Saves + shares | IG Insights |
| Profile link clicks | IG Insights |
| `project_created` same-day | app database |

Rules:
- A pillar that produces no link clicks for three weeks gets its slot reassigned.
- Any post in the top 20% by link clicks gets re-cut as a Meta ad. See
  `meta-ads.md` creative testing.
- Do not chase view counts. Pillar B will out-view Pillar A and under-convert it.
  That is expected; Pillar B exists to make the account worth following.
- Reuse the best posts. At week 9, re-post the top three from weeks 1 to 4 with a
  new hook rather than writing new ones.
