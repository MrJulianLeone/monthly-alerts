# Italy Partner Program

The Italy channel is not a paid-ads channel. It is a referral channel through the
professionals an American already hires when they buy and renovate a house in
Italy. Those professionals have the language problem worse than their clients do,
and they have it on every job.

Target: 15 to 25 active partners by month 6, each referring 2 to 6 paid projects a
year.

## 1. Partner ICP

Ranked by how directly they touch a renovation that has not started yet.

| Rank | Partner type | Why they refer | What they get out of it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Geometri** (surveyors/technical agents) | They are the operational hinge of every Italian renovation: permits, measurements, contractor coordination. They already field every English-language question badly. | Stops the WhatsApp translation load. Their client can read progress without calling them. |
| 2 | **Italian real estate brokers selling to Americans** | They hand off the client at closing and then get called for a year about renovation problems that are not their job. | A clean handoff, and a reason to stay in the client's life without doing unpaid work. |
| 3 | **Architects** (Ordine degli Architetti members) with foreign clients | Client communication is billable time they cannot bill. | "You'll receive a monthly project summary automatically" is a line they can put in a proposal. |
| 4 | **Renovation consultants / project managers for foreign buyers** | Their whole product is translation and coordination. | The checklist becomes their deliverable. Highest referral volume per partner. |
| 5 | **Property managers** handling second homes for foreign owners | They report to absent owners constantly. | The monthly status email replaces the report they write by hand. |
| 6 | **Imprese edili** (building firms) with foreign clients | They lose money on misunderstandings. | Fewer re-dos, fewer 6am phone calls. |
| 7 | **Relocation and residency consultants** | Renovation comes right after the purchase, and they get asked. | A referral they can make without liability. |
| 8 | **US-side agents and lawyers** specializing in Italian property purchases | They see the buyer before anyone in Italy does. | Earliest possible touchpoint. |

### Disqualifiers

- Anyone selling "1 euro house" packages or property-lead-generation content. Their
  audience is dreamers, not buyers.
- Agencies that want an exclusive, a white label, or a revenue share above the
  published credit.
- Anyone who wants to be paid to promote rather than paid per result.
- Large franchise brokerages where an individual agent cannot decide anything.

### Qualification signal

The partner must be able to name a current client who reads English and a current
job where the contractor does not. If they cannot, they are not a partner yet.

## 2. Where to find them

### Professional orders and registers (the highest-quality lists, all public)

| Source | What it is | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Collegio dei Geometri** provincial registers | Every province has a Collegio with a public member directory | Work province by province in high-foreign-buyer areas. Pull name, studio, email. |
| **Ordine degli Architetti** provincial registers | Same structure for architects | Filter for studios whose site has an English version. That English page is the qualification signal. |
| **Consiglio Nazionale Geometri** | National body, links to every provincial Collegio | Use it as the index for the above |
| **FIAIP / FIMAA** | Italian real estate agent federations with member directories | Search members in the target provinces |

### Target provinces (where Americans actually buy)

Tuscany (Firenze, Siena, Lucca, Grosseto), Umbria (Perugia), Puglia (Lecce,
Brindisi, Bari), Sicily (Ragusa, Siracusa, Palermo), Abruzzo (Chieti, Teramo),
Le Marche (Ascoli Piceno, Macerata), Lake Como (Como, Lecco), Piemonte (Cuneo,
Asti), Calabria (Cosenza, Reggio), Campania (Salerno).

### Brokers and agencies serving Americans

- Google in English: `buy house Tuscany American buyers`, `Italian real estate
  agency English speaking Puglia`, `property Italy for foreign buyers <province>`.
  The agencies that rank in English are the ones that market to Americans.
- **Gate-away.com** and **Italian real estate portals with English interfaces**:
  the listing agencies shown are self-selected for foreign clients.
- **International real estate networks** with Italian offices: Sotheby's
  International Realty, Christie's International Real Estate, Engel and Völkers,
  Great Estate Group, Casa and Country, Romolini Immobiliare.
- LinkedIn search (organic only, no paid): title `real estate` or `agente
  immobiliare`, location Italy, keyword `international clients` or `foreign
  buyers`.

### Expat and buyer communities (where the demand side lives, and where partners lurk)

- Facebook groups: `Americans Living in Italy`, `Expats in Italy`, `Renovating in
  Italy`, `Italy Property Buyers`, `Buying a House in Italy`, `Italian Dual
  Citizenship`, region-specific groups (`Expats in Puglia`, `Le Marche Expats`).
  Read the rules. Most ban promotion. Answer questions genuinely for four weeks
  before mentioning the product once.
- Forums: `expatsinitaly.com`, `italymagazine.com` community, r/ItalyExpats,
  r/juresanguinis (adjacent, high renovation intent).
- Substacks and YouTube channels documenting Italian renovations. These creators
  are partners, not ads. Offer them a free project on their own renovation.
- Comment threads under "we bought a house in Italy" content. The professionals
  answering questions there are the partners you want.

### Outreach volume

10 to 15 partner emails per week, hand-written, no automation. This list is small
and every recipient is findable. A templated blast to the Ordine degli Architetti
will get you talked about, in the wrong way.

## 4. Referral mechanics

### The link

Each partner gets a vanity referral link:

```
monthlyalerts.com/CODE
```

`CODE` is short, memorable, and belongs to the partner. Examples: `STUDIOROSSI`,
`GEOMBIANCHI`, `GREATESTATES`, `LUCCAARCH`. Letters and numbers only (4 to 20), uppercase in all written material,
case-insensitive in the app. Partners claim their own code in Settings once the
program is switched on (`REFERRALS_ENABLED=true`).

Behavior:
1. The link sets a referral cookie (90 days) and lands on the home page tagged
   `utm_source=referral`. Send partners the deep links they need alongside the
   code: `/renovating-in-italy` for brokers and consultants, `/for-designers` for
   architects, `/for-contractors` for imprese (the cookie is already set).
2. The referral code is written to the user row at signup, so attribution survives
   a device change.
3. When that user's project reaches `project_activated` (paid), the partner's
   credit balance increases.

### The credit

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Credit per paid project referred | **$25** |
| Credit per paid project during launch promotion (first 90 days of a partner's participation) | **$25** flat, no tiering |
| Minimum floor | $20 if the referred project was itself discounted |
| Accumulation | Credits add up with no expiry while the partner has referred at least one paid project in the trailing 12 months |
| Redemption | Credits apply against the partner's own project fees. Four referred projects fully pay for one of their own ($100). |
| Cash out | No cash payouts. Credit only. This keeps it simple, keeps it out of affiliate-tax territory, and keeps the partner using the product. |
| Self-referral | Not allowed. A partner's own projects do not earn credit. |
| Stacking | A referred client cannot also be given a free project. One incentive per project. |

### What the partner is told, verbatim

```
$20 credit for every paid project that comes through your link. Credits stack with
no expiry and pay for your own projects: four referrals cover one project of your
own, in full. There is no cash payout and there is nothing for you to invoice.
```

### Operations

- Monthly, on the 5th: pull referred activations from the app database grouped by
  referral code, update each partner's credit balance, and send one email per
  partner with the number.
- The partner email is three lines: how many projects came through their link last
  month, their new balance, and one sentence of thanks. No newsletter.
- If a partner refers zero projects in six months, send one honest email asking
  whether the offer is not landing with their clients, and what would change it.
  Then stop emailing them.
- Never pay for placement, a logo on their site, or a "featured partner" slot.
  Result-based only.
